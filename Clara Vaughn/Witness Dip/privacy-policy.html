Privacy Policy

App: Witness Dip
Developer: Clara Vaughn
Contact: clara.vaughn45@126.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Witness Dip is an offline iOS tool for a coating run. It stores layers and witness readings on the iPhone, and reports whether the open layer can shutter. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Layers and runs stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the report you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
