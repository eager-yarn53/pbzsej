Privacy Policy

App: Paste Release
Developer: Frank Keller
Contact: frank.keller62@sina.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Paste Release is an offline iOS tool for a stencil board. It stores pad widths and foil thickness on the iPhone, and names the pad that sticks worst. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Pads, foil, and saved runs stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the report you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
