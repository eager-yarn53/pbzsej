App: Folio Pair
Developer: Harold Crawford
Contact: harold.crawford72@163.com

Tap one folio, then tap another to swap their sewing order. The named folio is the first whose running sheet count passes the needle reach. Changing the reach recalculates the name and leaves the sewing order as it is. Export writes a text file of the current order on this iPhone.

Privacy. See the Privacy Policy linked from the App Store listing. No account is required.
