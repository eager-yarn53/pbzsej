Privacy Policy

App: Folio Pair
Developer: Harold Crawford
Contact: harold.crawford72@163.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Folio Pair is an offline iOS tool for sewing order. It stores folios and needle reach on the iPhone and names the first folio whose running sheet count passes the reach. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Folio names, sheet counts, reach, and saved runs stay on your iPhone. Export writes a text file on this iPhone and can hand it to the system share sheet. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
