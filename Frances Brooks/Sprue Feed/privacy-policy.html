Privacy Policy

App: Sprue Feed
Developer: Frances Brooks
Contact: frances.brooks83@sina.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Sprue Feed is an offline iOS tool for lost-wax sprue feeding. It stores wax patterns and sprue branches on the iPhone and names the largest pattern on the branch whose feed ratio is still above 1. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Patterns, superheat, and saved runs stay on your iPhone. A share sheet can send a text summary from this iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
