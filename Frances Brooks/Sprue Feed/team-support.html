App: Sprue Feed
Developer: Frances Brooks

Tap a wax pattern, then tap a sprue branch to hang it there. The named pattern is the largest one on the branch whose feed ratio is still above 1. Changing superheat recalculates the ratios and leaves each pattern on its branch. Shift moves the named pattern onto the branch with the most spare, and the name can move to another pattern.

Privacy. See the Privacy Policy linked from the App Store listing. No account is required.
