App: Ink Trap
Developer: Dorothy Sutton
Contact: dorothy.sutton44@sina.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Ink Trap is an offline iOS tool for plate trapping. It stores ink pairs on the iPhone and names the pair whose white gap is still the widest. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Pairs, squeeze, and saved runs stay on your iPhone. An export file can be shared from this iPhone with the system share sheet. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
