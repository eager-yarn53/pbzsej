Privacy Policy

App: Wedge Clock
Developer: Evelyn Yates
Contact: evelyn.yates81@sina.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Wedge Clock is an offline iOS tool for an optical shop. It stores wedges and clocking jobs on the iPhone, computes edge thickness at twelve hours, and reports how far the thin edge sits from a fixture mark. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Photo library. The photo library is used only when you attach a fixture photo to a wedge. The app keeps that image on the device.

Data on device. Wedges, jobs, and photos stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the report you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
