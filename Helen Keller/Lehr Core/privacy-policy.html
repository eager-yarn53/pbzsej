Privacy Policy

App: Lehr Core
Developer: Helen Keller
Contact: helen.keller67@126.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Lehr Core is an offline iOS tool for a glass annealing lehr. It stores section temperatures on the iPhone and names the section whose core is still hottest above strain. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Sections, strain, and saved runs stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Notifications. Saving a run can post a local notification on this iPhone. That notice is not sent to a server.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
